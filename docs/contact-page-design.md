## Design and scope

Contact extends the existing portfolio identity. The page uses the cream ground, dark green text and rules, rust field focus, Fraunces heading, Source Serif body, and established spacing tokens. It introduces no palette, font, imagery, dependency, or token-system change. The ignored local design record remains the incumbent authority.

The [first-party contact research](contact-page-research.md) informed a three-field inquiry form: Name, Email, Message. The page omits sales qualification and response-time promises. On desktop, the introduction occupies the left column and the ruled form occupies the wider right column. Below 48rem, the introduction precedes the stacked form. Name and Email share a row above that breakpoint. Fields have visible labels, required attributes, square borders, and rust keyboard focus.

The owner requires a private recipient. The preview includes no recipient address, mail link, delivery service, or successful-send claim. The disabled Send message control accompanies the explicit notice, “Design preview. Messages are not sent.” Delivery needs a configured and verified server-side submission path before release. A future implementation must keep its recipient out of browser assets.

Contact appears in the shared navigation and the seven preserved project-page headers. The render-time header addition and scoped wrapping rule preserve the immutable project exports. The original project assets, analytics production guard, and hosting policies remain intact.
