# Contact page references

Checked 7 November 2026 against first-party pages. Scope: six contact experiences across visualization tools, data journalism, a visualization portfolio, and sports analytics, with Observable's contact guidance as a seventh supporting reference. No messages were sent and no forms were submitted. The observations below describe page content and its order; they do not establish rendered column widths, mobile behavior, keyboard access, or delivery success.

## Recommendation

Use a short Contact page with Name, Email, Message, and Send. Keep the recipient private in server-side configuration. Do not publish an address or offer a mail link or exposed-address alternative. This follows Narayan's clarified privacy requirement. Datawrapper's sales form supplies a compact field pattern; its optional Organization field adds no stated value here. [Datawrapper sales](https://www.datawrapper.de/contact/sales)

For this portfolio, one person receiving the messages does not need separate press, sales, support, or partnership destinations. Give the form the visual emphasis, with a short page heading above it. Preserve the existing cream ground, dark green type, Fraunces heading, Source Serif body, and IBM Plex Mono labels where they help. Use the established band spacing and desktop offsets. Do not import another site's illustrations, client logos, testimonials, text, type, or palette. These are adaptations to the portfolio's supplied design constraints, not observations about the reference sites.

Delivery needs a configured mail service and a verified server-side submission path before release. A visual form must not claim that a message was sent until the service accepts it. The recipient must not appear in browser-delivered HTML, JavaScript, or form configuration.

## Supporting reference: Observable

Observable's FAQ routes bugs and help to topic-specific public forums, company-plan questions to a sales address, and account-deletion help to a support address. These appear as questions and answers within documentation, without an embedded contact form in that section. This is contact guidance rather than a dedicated contact page. Its useful lesson is to state which route serves which question when multiple destinations exist. It supplies no reason to add those destinations to a one-person portfolio. [Observable FAQ: User support and feedback](https://observablehq.github.io/documentation/faq)

## Decisions the research does not settle

- **Recipient:** Narayan must configure the recipient in server-side settings. No address was inferred from GitHub, a domain, or a naming convention, and none belongs in public page assets.
- **Form delivery:** These examples do not establish which service this site should use. A visual form without a configured and tested destination must not show a successful-send claim.
- **Public copy:** No claims about availability, employment interests, response times, location, or preferred inquiry types follow from these references.
- **Layout quality:** Content extraction establishes headings, labels, links, and order. It does not justify claims about visual alignment, spacing, responsive layout, accessibility, or conversion performance.

Our World in Data's attempted contact URLs were inaccessible through the research tool, so its contact experience was excluded from the comparison. Flourish and Hudl exposed contact instructions but not form controls; those rows preserve that uncertainty. No delivery, spam filtering, validation, or error-state tests were performed.
