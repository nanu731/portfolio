## The model

The production Bayesian conditional autoregressive model estimates a separate
2025-26 shooting surface for each of 318 players. The relocation calculation removes
attempt mass from a player's weakest modeled locations first, then allocates it across
supported destinations. No destination can finish above 50% of the player's attempts.

The calculation chooses source locations without using whether a shot went in. A
destination needs at least 10 attempts and 90% posterior certainty that it exceeds
the player's current weighted expected-points baseline. One supported destination can
produce an estimate; the 50% cap can stop relocation below the requested amount.

## What the numbers mean

The explorer reports posterior means with 90% intervals. The season figure uses each
player's observed 2025-26 attempt volume; the per-100 figure gives a common scale.
The Shot Selection Score applies the self-relative formula to the 25% targeted result.
It does not represent league rank or overall player quality.

The evidence rule finds no supported destination for 29 players, one for 167 players,
and two or more for 122 players. The page publishes relocation estimates for 276
players. Thirteen single-destination players have no room under the 50% cap, so their
scores and gains remain unavailable rather than appearing as zero.

</Band>

## Limits

These are modeled, non-causal estimates based on past shots. They do not guarantee
improvement. The model excludes whether a new shot can be created, how defenses
respond, fatigue, passing, shot-clock pressure, and game context.

</Band>

## Definitions

### Shot Selection Score

This 0–100 score compares a player's modeled points per shot in the historical mix
with the feasible result at the 25% request. A score of 90 means the historical mix
produces about 90% of the modeled points per shot available in this scenario; it does
not mean the player ranks in the 90th percentile.

### Maximum Relocated Share

A 25% setting allows at most 25 of every 100 attempt-equivalents to move. The actual
amount can be lower when weak source shots run out or the 50% destination cap binds.

### Season Gain

A value of +157 means the model estimates 157 additional points over the same season
shot volume. The estimate keeps the player's number of attempts fixed.

### Gain per 100 shots

A value of +17 means an estimated 17 additional points per 100 attempts. This common
scale removes differences in player shot volume, but the comparison remains non-causal.

### 90% interval

A range such as 129–183 contains the middle 90% of the model's estimates under its
data and assumptions. It does not guarantee that a future result will fall in that range.

### Single-destination estimate

Only one court cell passed the 10-attempt and 90%-certainty evidence rule. Relocation
stops when that cell reaches the universal 50% cap.

### Supported destination

A supported destination is a court cell with at least 10 attempts and at least 90%
posterior certainty of beating the player's current weighted shot mix.
