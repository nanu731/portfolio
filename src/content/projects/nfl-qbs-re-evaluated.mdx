## Persistence has limits

Annual CPOE association was **0.507**, compared with **0.452** for completion percentage on the same throws. Their difference was **0.055**, with a conditional interval from **−0.274 to 0.384**. Omitting one paired player reverses that difference. This evidence does not establish CPOE superiority.

Recorded sack avoidance had a primary interleaved association of **0.265**, with a conditional interval from **−0.289 to 0.685**. Chronological and annual results answer separate questions; the study view retains all three.

Run contributions combine frequency with shared observed value. Primary scramble association was **0.313 [−0.190, 0.686]**; non-scramble run association was **0.697 [0.004, 0.938]**. Annual associations were **0.727 [0.226, 0.924]** and **0.429 [−0.080, 0.761]**, respectively. Broad intervals and shared circumstances limit enduring-skill claims. The charts retain all four endpoints.

## Read the denominators

CPOE describes completion success above the supplied model expectation. Completion percentage uses the same eligible throws. EPA describes shared possession-team play value, including other participants and circumstances.

Recorded dropbacks **D** include played passes, sacks and scramble-runs. Combined opportunities **O** add recorded non-scramble player runs **R** to D. R includes aborted plays and laterals; intent remains unknown. Scrambles already belong to D. A contribution is the role's total EPA divided by O, while per-run EPA uses only that role's run count. Do not add these O-based contributions to D-based EPA.

Arm strength and processing remain **unmeasured**. A context-adjusted overall score remains **unavailable**. These overlapping proxies do not establish independent traits, isolated mobility, rankings or forecasts. Persistent support, survivor selection, rare events, serial dependence and unknown vintage of the supplied completion-probability model remain limitations.

## Sources and method

<details>
<summary>Methods, uncertainty and remaining gaps</summary>

The cohort requires at least 200 accepted eligible throws and ten eligible games in **each** year. It selects surviving, regular-use passers through supplied completion probability (CP) data availability; it does not represent all NFL quarterbacks or certify player positions.

The analysis uses 2023 and 2024 regular-season nflverse play-by-play. Player estimates use fixed opportunity definitions and cohort membership across team changes. CPOE and completion use the same frozen eligible throws (E). Dropback and combined-opportunity populations have no CP filter. Sparse recorded runs remain visible with their counts. Cohort membership stays fixed; the analysis does not impute missing values, clip outcomes or shrink estimates.

Player EPA and run-contribution bars show approximate 95% marginal conditional game-sampling sensitivities. They do not test player differences, ranks or season changes. No individual CPOE, completion or sack-avoidance intervals were estimated. Collapsed limits mean no observed deletion variation, not certainty. Zero recorded role events give zero share and contribution, with unavailable per-run EPA; missing evidence is never converted to zero.

Study intervals delete whole contributing NFL games. Games serve as deletion/sampling units. The annual strata contain 223 + 208 games where reported; they do not form 431 independent quarterback pairs or repeated validation studies. Approximate independence between games remains a working assumption. CPOE and sack/run studies use different accepted variance centering rules. The study table keeps those rules and all ten results visible. Overlapping data do not support comparisons of statistical significance across studies.

The historical expansion remains blocked by unresolved source conflicts in 2011. These charts use only the accepted 2023 and 2024 studies.
