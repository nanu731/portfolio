## What these measures can tell us

CPOE describes completion success above the supplied model expectation. Completion percentage uses the same eligible throws. EPA describes shared possession-team play value, including other participants and circumstances.

These measures do not establish isolated skill or a context-adjusted overall score. Persistent supporting circumstances, survivor selection and conditional uncertainty limit interpretation.

## Sources and method

The analysis uses 2023 and 2024 regular-season nflverse play-by-play. Player estimates use fixed opportunity definitions and cohort membership. Study-level association intervals and marginal player intervals answer different questions.
